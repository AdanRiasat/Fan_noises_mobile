# Custom Fan and White Noise

A collection of calming white noise and fan sounds, plus the ability to add your own recordings for a truly personal sleep experience.

## Features

### Play Sound

The app comes with pre-existing audio (different fans and white noise) at different levels. Pressing play starts the selected audio, which plays infinitely. The audio file itself is very short and simply loops indefinitely with gapless playback.

All audio files are in .wav format specifically for gapless playback.

### Ads with AdMob

With Google AdMob, the app displays a banner ad at the bottom of the screen and an interstitial ad that pops up at times, such as when pausing audio or when the timer runs out.

A consent form is issued in regions that require it for ads to run.

All ads are muted, or contain no volume, so as not to wake up the user if they're using the timer feature and an ad plays while they're sleeping.

### Timer

Users can set a timer for how long the audio should run. When the timer ends, so does the audio.

### Visuals

When playing audio, the fan blades spin; when the audio is paused, the fan blades stop. There's also an acceleration and deceleration effect on the blades when starting and stopping the audio.

### Custom Audio

On the "Manage Fans" page, users can add their own audio to be played like the other pre-existing audio. The audio will automatically loop when played.

It is recommended to use audio formats such as WAV or FLAC to avoid silence at the beginning or end of the audio file.
