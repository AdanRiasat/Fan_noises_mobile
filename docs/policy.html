Privacy Policy

Custom Fan and White Noise  ·  Last updated: 2 August 2026

Custom Fan and White Noise does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Everything you create in the app stays on your device. The only company that receives any data is Google, through the advertising shown in the app — and only with your consent where the law requires it.

This policy explains how the Custom Fan and White Noise Android application (package com.adan.custom_fan_white_noise, the “App”) handles your information. It applies only to this App.

1. Information the App stores on your device

The App saves the following locally, in its own private storage. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else, and we have no ability to see it:

  • Your fan list — the names of the fan and white-noise sounds you create, and whether each one is built in or added by you.
  • Audio files you import — when you add your own sound, the App copies the file you selected into its private storage so it can be played back later. The copy never leaves your device.

Choosing a file happens through your device's own file picker. The App does not browse your storage and receives only the single file you pick.

2. Advertising

The App displays ads supplied by Google AdMob. This is the only way any data about you leaves your device. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID (a resettable identifier)
  • your IP address and approximate, country-level location derived from it
  • device and app information — such as device model, operating system version, language, and the fact that the ad was shown in this App
  • ad interaction data — whether an ad was viewed or clicked

Google acts as an independent controller of this data. Its handling is governed by Google's own policies, not ours:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

Your choices about ads

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before any ads are loaded, using Google's certified consent management platform. If you decline, the advertising SDK is not initialised at all and no advertising data is collected.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time: open the menu in the App and tap “Ad privacy options”.

Independently of the App, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation entirely, in Settings → Privacy → Ads on your Android device.

3. What the App does not do

  • No account, sign-in, or registration.
  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No crash reporting.
  • No collection of your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.
  • No microphone or camera access.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information with third parties for money or for cross-context behavioural advertising, beyond the advertising described in section 2.

4. Permissions

Permission Why it is needed
INTERNET To download the ads shown in the App. The App has no other network features.
com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID Added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK so ads can be served and measured using your device's advertising ID.

5. Data retention and deletion

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing on our side to retain or delete.

  • Individual sounds: delete a fan in the App and its entry and imported audio file are removed.
  • Everything: uninstalling the App permanently deletes its database and all imported audio from your device.
  • Advertising data held by Google: managed through your Google account and device settings — see My Ad Center.

6. Security

Data the App stores is kept in Android's app-private storage, which the operating system isolates from other apps. Ad requests are transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections by the Google Mobile Ads SDK. As we operate no servers and receive no user data, there is no server-side store that could be breached.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, please contact us and we will address it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the CCPA/CPRA, or similar laws — including rights of access, correction, deletion, and objection to processing.

We cannot act on such requests ourselves, because we do not hold any information that identifies you. Requests relating to advertising data should be directed to Google using the links in section 2. Data stored by the App is entirely under your control and can be deleted as described in section 5. If you have questions about any of this, contact us using the details below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the App changes. The revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated “last updated” date. Material changes will be reflected in the App's store listing.
